Hospital

Doctors, patients and visits in one place

What the module does

  • Doctors with categories, mentors and interns; a kanban grouped by category.
  • Patients with a personal doctor and its change history.
  • Visits with planned / done / cancelled states, calendar, pivot and graph views.
  • Hierarchical disease classifier with translatable names.
  • PDF "Doctor Report": visit history and patients of each doctor.
  • Wizards: visit report, disease report, mass reassignment of the personal doctor.
  • Daily cron that cancels overdue planned visits.

Access rights

Five groups in the Hospital section of user settings, each one including the previous:

GroupVisits
Patientreads own visits
Internreads and edits own visits
Doctorreads and edits own visits and visits of own interns
Managerreads and edits all visits, cannot delete
Administratorfull access, including deletion

Internal users outside these groups do not see the module data.

Languages

English and Ukrainian (interface and disease names).
